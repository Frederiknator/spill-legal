Privacy Policy

Spill (mobile game) · Effective 7 November 2026

Who is responsible

Spill is made and published by Frederik Bjørling Bornemann, a private individual in Denmark (“I”, “me”). I am the data controller for the personal data described here.

Contact: frederikbornemann@gmail.com

The short version

  • Spill has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress, stars, settings and skins are stored only on your device.
  • Spill shows ads from Google AdMob. In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; without consent you only get non-personalised ads.
  • The optional “Remove ads” purchase is handled by Apple or Google, and verified through RevenueCat.
  • Spill uses no analytics service: nothing reports how you play to me or to anyone else, and Spill does not sell your data. The Stats screen in the game is worked out from the save on your device and never leaves it. (The ads SDK measures ad views and taps for Google; see Advertising below.)

Data stored on your device

The game saves your level results, stars, Unlimited scores, play statistics (shown on the Stats screen), settings and skins in the app's own storage on your device. This data never leaves your device through Spill and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Spill shows ads between levels and, only when you choose to watch one, rewarded ads (for a hint, extra stars or more time). The “Remove ads” purchase turns off the ads between levels; rewarded ads stay available if you choose to watch them. Ads are provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC (“AdMob”).

To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, only if you allow tracking; Android Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • Consent. If you are in the EU/EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form (a certified consent management platform) before personalised ads are used. You can change your choice at any time in Settings.
  • Legal basis. Personalised ads: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Non-personalised ads, ad delivery, fraud prevention and measurement: legitimate interest in funding a free game (Art. 6(1)(f)), and Google's own legal bases as described in its policies.
  • iOS. On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only used if you allow it in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Profiling and choice. Personalised ads involve profiling by Google (choosing ads from your interests), only with your consent. Spill makes no automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Giving consent is optional: if you don't, you still get the full game, with non-personalised ads.

Google's processing is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

If you buy “Remove ads”, the payment is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); I never see your payment details. To confirm the purchase and restore it on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., which receives the purchase receipt, a random app user ID created on your device, the product bought, and basic device and app information. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).

App stores and this website

Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) process data about your download, updates and payments as independent controllers under their own privacy policies. These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

International transfers

Google, RevenueCat and GitHub may process data outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States. They rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long data is kept

Data on your device: until you delete the app. Data held by Google and RevenueCat: according to their retention policies linked above. I do not keep any copy of your data myself.

Children

Spill is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact me and I will help have it removed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Ad privacy choices). Because I hold no data about you myself, most requests are best directed to Google or RevenueCat, but you are welcome to contact me and I will help.

You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.dk) or the authority in your own country.

Changes
